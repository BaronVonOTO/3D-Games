Dusk Dunes

Click to look around. W A S D or arrow keys to walk. Shift to run. Esc to pause.

On iPhone: you always move forward. Turn your body or tilt the phone to look and steer. Touch and hold to brake, tap to jump. Try the Cardboard view in a headset.

Press M to change view while walking, [ and ] to change depth.

Multiplayer: host a game and share the 4-letter code.
