Dusk Dunes

Click to look around. W A S D or arrow keys to walk. Shift to run. Esc to pause.

On iPhone: you always move forward. Tilt the phone or swipe to steer. Touch and hold to brake, tap to jump. Flick the phone up to jump, down to dive.

Full screen: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from your home screen to hide the browser bars.

Effects (stack with any view, and each other)

Press M to change view while walking, [ and ] to change depth.

Multiplayer: host a game and share the 4-letter code.
